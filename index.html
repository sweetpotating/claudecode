How to Play

4D

  • Choose any 4-digit number from 0000 to 9999.
  • Minimum bet: S$1 per entry.
  • Each draw picks 23 winning numbers: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 10 Starter, 10 Consolation.
  • Bet types: Ordinary (Big covers all 23 prizes; Small covers top 3), iBet (wins if any permutation matches), 4D Roll (one digit is a wildcard 0-9), System Entry (all 24 permutations of 4 unique digits).
  • Draws: Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday at 6:30 PM.

Toto

  • Pick 6 numbers from 1 to 49. Minimum bet: S$1.
  • Each draw: 6 winning numbers plus 1 additional number (for Groups 2 & 5).
  • System entries: pick 7-12 numbers to cover every 6-number combination from your set (higher cost, higher coverage).
  • System Roll: pick 5 numbers plus a rolling wildcard.
  • Draws: Monday and Thursday at 6:30 PM.
